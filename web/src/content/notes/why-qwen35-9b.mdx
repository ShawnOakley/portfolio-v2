# Why Qwen3.5 9B

**Decision.** The support verdicts use Qwen3.5 9B, because that is the model
the checker was scored with.

**Why.** A larger Qwen or Claude would be a different result, not a
continuation of that one. The page sends one id, `qwen3.5-9b`. Verdicts stay
off until the visitor asks for them.

**What I rejected.**

- _Running the 27B model._
- _Calling Claude before a key existed._
- _Substituting a free model from another family._
