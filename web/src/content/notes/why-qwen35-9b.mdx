# Why Qwen3.5 9B

Support verdicts run on Qwen3.5 9B because that is the model I scored the
checker with. The numbers on the cite-checker page describe that model and no
other.

A larger Qwen, or Claude, might do better. It would also be a different
result, and I would have to score it again before saying anything about it.
Until then the page offers the one model, and verdicts stay off unless you ask
for them.
