# Why one check is one request

**Decision.** `POST /v1/check` takes the whole brief and returns one report.

**Why.** Citation lookup already sends up to 60 citations in one
CourtListener call.

**What I rejected.**

- _Cutting the brief into sections and sending those sections in parallel._
  Parallel sections share one CourtListener allowance of 5 requests a minute.
- _Streaming the report._ A stream still dies when the function reaches 5
  minutes.

**Cost.** A short brief finishes. A long brief with support verdicts may not.
